Important pilot rules represented in these forms:
- Introductory labor rate: $30/hour.
- Customer-specific travel time: $30/hour.
- Rideshare: actual documented fare + 20% tip.
- Review Before Delete.
- Original physical records stay on-site during pilot.
- Temporary-data countdown begins when final delivery is successfully sent or made available through the agreed method; customer acknowledgment is not required.
- Payment terms are deliberately selectable during the dry run rather than permanently locked yet.
- Customer-facing legal/authorization language is a pilot operational draft and should receive appropriate legal review before broader use.
